Missing & Special Marks Form

For students with missing marks or special exam results.

Submit before the deadline. After that the form closes and your request can’t be included in what goes to lecturers.

About this form

Use this form if a result for one of your units is missing from your portal or results slip (a missing mark, or a special exam result that was never posted). Your class representatives will collect all submissions, print them and send them to the relevant lecturers.

Have ready:

  • Your registration number and full name
  • Your school/faculty and department
  • For each unit: the unit code (letters and numbers only) and name, your lecturer’s name, and the semester

How to fill it in:

  1. Enter your details and pick your school and department. If yours isn’t listed, choose “Not listed – add new”.
  2. Add each unit with a missing result. Use “Add another unit” for more than one.
  3. Choose the semester. The exam period fills in automatically.
  4. Check everything, then press Submit.

Please tell your classmates and friends in other classes and schools who have missing marks. Everyone must submit their own form before the deadline.
