Missing & Special Marks Form

For students with missing marks or special exam results.

Deadline: Tuesday, 10 November 2026 at 1:00 PM

Submit before the deadline. After that the form closes and your request can’t be included in what goes to lecturers.

About this form

Use this form if a result for one of your units is missing from your portal or results slip (a missing mark, or a special exam result that was never posted). Your class representatives will collect all submissions, print them and send them to the relevant lecturers so they can look into each case.

Before you start, have ready:

  • Your registration number and full name
  • Your school/faculty and department
  • For each unit: the unit code and name (e.g. BIT456 Data Engineering), your lecturer’s name, and the semester

How to fill it in:

  1. Enter your details and pick your school and department. If yours isn’t listed, choose “Not listed – add new” (a school needs at least one department).
  2. Add each unit with a missing result. Use “Add another unit” for more than one.
  3. Choose the semester (Sem 1 2026 or Sem 2 2026). The exam period fills in automatically.
  4. Check everything, then press Submit.

Please tell your classmates and friends in other classes and schools who have missing marks. Everyone has to submit their own form before the deadline.
